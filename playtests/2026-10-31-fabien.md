### C2. Fabien's game freezes in our code (five times, Windows only)

- **Seen:** every one of Fabien's "crashes" during play. The watchdog's dump shows the main thread blocked inside

  `+0x30540b`) ← `+0x30480b` ← `+0x3048d3` ← trampoline.
- **One of our threads in every dump:** it's blocked with `+0x2b66b5…+0xd01ef0` on its stack (thread start
  `+0xdb8ee5`). It looks like a wait on that thread, or on a lock it holds.
- **When each freeze started** (the watchdog reports 10 s later), **and what Fabien's game was doing:**
  - 14:33:22: returning to the main menu after 3 failed reconnects (your game had crashed). Export `2036-c0b`.
  - 14:41:56: mid-play as guest, `GuestHands` moving `FF5D2`/`FF5D9` every 5 s. Export `2044-915`. Your game
    timed him out at 14:42:28.
  - 14:57:47: party travel into Riften after the jail topic (S3). Export `2100-4ea`.
  - 15:09:51: loading the save your game sent (your load at 15:09:24). Export `2115-567`.
  - 15:38:31: as host, at 0.3 FPS (P1), 3 s after your game crashed on quit (C3). Export `2141-606`.
- **Not freezes:** the 10 s watchdog stalls while loading saves (14:28, 14:30, 14:38, 14:48, 15:00, 15:25,
  15:40) recovered and have engine frames. They're just slow loads (P2).
- **Linux:** your game never froze this way.
- **Next:** a package with a kept PDB, then replay one trigger on Windows (the plan from the last session:
  reconnect failure → main menu, or a guest loading the host's save). The offsets will name the wait.

### C3. Crash on quit: Papyrus VM teardown (4 times, both OSes)

- **Seen:**
  - Fabien 14:27:33 (`2029-33e`, dump) and 14:35:47 (`2037-0a3`, dump).
  - Yours 15:38:28 (`reports/2026-10-31-1538-1a7-crash/`) and 15:41:01 (`.dmp` in the game folder).
- **Stack (identical each time):** our `+0x8e91e4` ← `+0x93f0c8` ← `+0x93efe8` → AV at `+0xac8e6b` (or

- **How players see it:** closing the game after a freeze or a disconnect shows a crash. It inflates the crash
  count, but no play is lost.
